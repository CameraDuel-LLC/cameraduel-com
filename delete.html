You can permanently delete your CameraDuel account and personal data at any time — directly from the app.

Delete from the app

Open CameraDuel and go to Settings → Account → Delete account, then confirm. That's it — no form to fill out and no waiting period.

What gets deleted

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your account and login
  • Your profile — name, username, email, date of birth, and profile photo
  • Your friend connections and contact-matching data
  • Your streak and other personal account data

What stays

A duel is something you share with your friends. Photos you already submitted into a duel may remain visible to the friends you dueled with, as part of their saved history — much like a message you've already sent to someone. This is the only content that isn't removed, and it is never used for advertising or shared outside those duels.

Can't open the app?

If you can no longer access the app, email us at admin@cameraduel.com from the email address on your account and we'll delete it for you. Please allow a few days for us to verify and process the request.

Questions about deletion or your data? See our Privacy Policy or reach us at admin@cameraduel.com.
