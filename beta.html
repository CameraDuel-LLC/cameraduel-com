What this is

CameraDuel is a small daily ritual for keeping up with the friends you don't want to lose touch with. Every day everyone gets the same prompt — you take a photo, your friends take theirs, and you all see how you each saw it. No feed to scroll, no first move to agonize over. Just one real moment a day.
