Stuck on something, found a bug, or just have a question? We're a small team and we read every message.

Contact us

Email admin@cameraduel.com. Include the email on your account and, if something's broken, your phone model and app version — it helps us fix things faster. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

I can't log in

Double-check that you're using the email you signed up with. If you're still locked out, email us from that address and we'll help you get back in.

My friends aren't showing up

Make sure they've joined CameraDuel and that you've allowed contacts access in your phone's Settings. You can also add friends by username.

I'm not getting notifications

Check that notifications are enabled for CameraDuel in your phone's Settings, and that Do Not Disturb or Focus modes aren't silencing them.

Something in the app is broken

Try updating to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play, then restart the app. If the problem continues, email us with what happened and we'll look into it.

How do I report a user or a photo?

Email us with the username and a description of what happened. We review every report.
